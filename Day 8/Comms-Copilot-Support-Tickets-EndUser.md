# Copilot Support — What's Happening & Next Steps

Plain-English explanations for each reported issue, with the steps you can take next.

---

## Ticket 1 — Q3 board pack in SharePoint won't summarise

**What's happening:** When a file is very new or was recently changed, it can take a little while for Microsoft's systems to "read" and index it before Copilot is able to use it. It's also possible the way the file was shared with you (a link, rather than direct access) isn't recognised by Copilot in the same way.

**Next steps:**
- Wait a short while (up to a few hours) and try again, especially if the file was uploaded or edited recently.
- Check how you were given access to the file — if it was via a shared link, ask the file owner to add you directly instead.
- If it still doesn't work after this, log a ticket with IT and include the file name, link, and when it was last updated.

---

## Ticket 2 — New starter: Copilot in Outlook doesn't know about recent emails

**What's happening:** This is completely normal for a brand-new account. When an account is first created, it takes some time for your mailbox and licence to fully activate and for Copilot to build up an understanding of your content.

**Next steps:**
- No action needed right away — this typically resolves within a day or two as your account settles in.
- If it's still not working after a few working days, contact IT to confirm your Copilot licence is active.

---

## Ticket 3 — HR manager: Copilot says "I don't have access" to a sensitive salary spreadsheet

**What's happening:** This isn't a fault — it's Copilot working as intended. Sensitive files (like salary data) are often protected with a label that deliberately blocks AI tools from reading them, to keep the data secure.

**Next steps:**
- This is expected behaviour for highly sensitive files and won't be "fixed" — Copilot is not meant to access this content.
- If you believe you should have AI-assisted access to this file, speak to your data/information security team about the file's sensitivity label and whether an exception is appropriate.

---

## Ticket 4 — Sales rep: Copilot can't find a client contract shared via a guest link from another company

**What's happening:** Copilot is designed to work with content inside your own organisation. Files shared with you from another company's system (via a guest link) generally sit outside what Copilot can search, even though you can open and view them yourself.

**Next steps:**
- Try downloading the contract and saving a copy into your own organisation's SharePoint or OneDrive — Copilot will then be able to find and use it.
- For files you'll need Copilot's help with regularly, ask the sender to share via your own organisation's systems where possible.

---

## Ticket 5 — IT admin: Copilot stopped working for the whole Finance team overnight

**What's happening:** When an issue suddenly affects a whole team at once, it's usually caused by a change made overnight (like a licence update or a security policy change) rather than Copilot itself breaking. It's also worth checking if there's a wider Microsoft service issue.

**Next steps:**
- Check the Microsoft 365 Service Health page for any reported outages affecting Copilot.
- Check whether any licence, group membership, or security policy changes were made for the Finance team recently.
- If neither of these explains it, raise directly with Microsoft support as a potential service issue.

---

## Ticket 6 — Manager: Copilot summarised a file from a folder they don't remember having access to

**What's happening:** This isn't a fault — Copilot only ever shows you files you already have permission to see, even if you'd forgotten about that access (for example, through a team or group you belong to).

**Next steps:**
- No action needed — this is expected behaviour, confirming you do have legitimate access to that folder.
- If you're concerned you shouldn't have access to that folder, contact IT or the file owner to review and adjust your permissions.

---

## Ticket 7 — Analyst: Copilot gives generic answers and doesn't seem to use our SharePoint content

**What's happening:** This usually means Copilot either can't find your team's content in its search index yet, or you don't have direct access to the SharePoint sites where that content lives.

**Next steps:**
- Try searching for the same content directly in SharePoint search — if that also doesn't find it, the issue is with access or indexing, not Copilot itself.
- Confirm with your site owner that you have access to the relevant SharePoint sites/libraries.
- If SharePoint search works fine but Copilot still doesn't use the content, log a ticket with IT including examples of what you searched for.

---

## Ticket 8 — Executive assistant: Copilot in Outlook can't see a shared mailbox calendar managed on behalf of a director

**What's happening:** Copilot in Outlook is currently designed to work with your own mailbox only. Even though you have delegate access to manage another mailbox's calendar, Copilot doesn't yet extend into shared or delegated mailboxes.

**Next steps:**
- Continue managing the shared calendar directly in Outlook as usual — this is a current limitation, not something that can be fixed on your account.
- If this is a common need in your team, log a ticket with IT so they can track it as a feature request/limitation, but no fix is expected for individual accounts.
