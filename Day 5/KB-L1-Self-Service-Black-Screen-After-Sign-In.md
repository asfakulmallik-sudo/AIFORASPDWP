# Black Screen After Log In: Quick Help

Version: v1.0  
Date: 07/08/2026  
Status: Draft

If your work screen opens to a black screen, do not worry. Your files and company information are safe. This problem usually clears after you try again.

## What you can do right now

1. Wait 30 to 60 seconds once after you log in.  
Expected: Your work screen appears normally.

2. If the screen is still black, log out fully from the work screen.  
Expected: You return to the normal log in page.

3. Close the work desktop program completely, then open it again.  
Expected: A new log in starts.

4. Log in again one time.  
Expected: Your work screen opens normally.

5. If it still does not work, restart your laptop or PC once.  
Expected: A restart often fixes temporary log in problems.

6. After restart, log in to the work desktop again.  
Expected: You can continue working.

7. If the problem is still there, stop trying again and contact the IT Help Desk.  
Expected: Support can move you to a working setup quickly.

## Important reassurance

- Your documents, email, and personal work settings are stored safely.
- A black screen does not mean your files were deleted.
- Avoid trying to log in many times quickly; this can slow down recovery.

## Details to include in your request

Please share these details to speed up support:

- Your full name and work log in name
- Best phone number to reach you
- Exact time the problem started
- What you see (for example: "black screen after log in")
- How long you waited before logging out
- Whether you already restarted your laptop or PC
- Your location (office/home) and internet type (Wi-Fi/cable/hotspot)
- Computer name (if known)
- Picture of the screen (if possible)

Contact the IT Help Desk if the screen stays black after one log out and log in attempt and one device restart, or if you cannot work.
