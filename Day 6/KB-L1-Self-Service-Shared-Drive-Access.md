# Cannot Access the Finance Shared Drive: Quick Help

Version: v1.0
Date: 2026-08-10
Status: Draft

If you get an "Access is denied" message or a red X on your Finance shared drive, do not worry. Your files have not been deleted or changed. This is usually a sign-in permissions issue that clears once you refresh your sign-in.

## What you can do right now

1. Sign out of your laptop or PC completely, then sign back in.
Expected: A fresh sign-in usually restores access right away.

2. After signing back in, try opening the Finance shared drive again.
Expected: The drive opens normally and you can see your files.

3. If you are working from home or remotely, disconnect and reconnect your remote/VPN session, then sign in again.
Expected: This refreshes your access the same way a normal sign-out/sign-in does.

4. If the drive still shows a red X, right-click it and choose to reconnect or refresh, then try opening it again.
Expected: The drive reconnects and opens normally.

5. If it still does not work after one sign-out/sign-in, stop trying repeatedly and contact the IT Help Desk.
Expected: Support can check your account access and fix it directly.

## Important reassurance

- Your files on the Finance shared drive are safe and have not been lost or changed.
- This issue is related to account permissions refreshing, not a problem with your documents.
- Repeated sign-in attempts will not fix it faster and may make troubleshooting harder — one sign-out/sign-in is enough to try.

## Details to include in your request

Please share these details to speed up support:

- Your full name and work sign-in name
- Best phone number to reach you
- Exact time you first saw the problem
- What you see (for example: "Access is denied" or a red X on the drive)
- Whether you have already tried signing out and back in
- Your location (office/home) and connection type (office network/VPN)
- Which team within Finance you are part of
- A picture of the error message, if possible

Contact the IT Help Desk if the shared drive is still unavailable after one full sign-out and sign-in.
