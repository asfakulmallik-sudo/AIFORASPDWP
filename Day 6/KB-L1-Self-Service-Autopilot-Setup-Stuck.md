# Your Laptop Setup (Autopilot) Won't Finish: Quick Help

Version: v1.0
Date: 2026-08-11
Status: Draft

If your new or reset laptop is stuck during company setup, or shows an error while signing in for the first time, do not worry. Your account and files are safe. This is a device setup issue, not a problem with your data.

## What you can do right now

1. Wait a few minutes on the setup screen without pressing anything.
Expected: Setup often continues on its own after a short delay.

2. If the screen shows an error and a "Retry" or "Try again" option, select it once.
Expected: Setup restarts and may complete successfully.

3. If setup still fails, restart the laptop once and let it go through the setup screens again from the start.
Expected: A restart often clears a temporary setup problem.

4. Do not keep signing in repeatedly or restarting multiple times in a row.
Expected: Repeated attempts do not speed up setup and can make it harder for IT to diagnose.

5. If it still does not complete after one restart, stop and contact the IT Help Desk with the laptop nearby (or on a call so they can see the screen).
Expected: Support can check your device's account setup and fix it directly.

## Important reassurance

- Your company account, email, and files are safe and are not affected by this setup issue.
- This is normally caused by a leftover setup record from before your laptop was prepared for you, not anything you did.
- One restart and one retry is enough to try before contacting support — repeating it further will not help.

## Details to include in your request

Please share these details to speed up support:

- Your full name and work sign-in name
- Best phone number to reach you
- The exact error message or code shown on screen (a photo of the screen helps)
- Whether this is a brand-new laptop or one that was reset/reused
- Whether you have already tried restarting once
- Your location (office/home) and connection type (office network/VPN/hotspot)
- Computer name or asset tag (if visible on the device or a label)
