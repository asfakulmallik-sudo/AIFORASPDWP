# Leadership Update: Win11 Migration — Executive Summary

Date: 2026-08-12
Status: Draft
Audience: Non-technical leadership

---

Overall, the Windows 11 migration has gone well for most staff. The early problems we saw in the first week — people being unable to sign in, unstable remote connections, and files not showing up as expected — have all been resolved, and staff feedback this week confirms things are working normally or better, with several people specifically calling out how much smoother their experience has become. Confidence in the migration is trending in the right direction, and there's no indication of a repeat of those early issues.

That said, two things remain open and need continued attention. First, the printer issue on the third floor has not been fixed and is now in its third week — frustration in that team is growing, and we are treating this as a priority to resolve properly rather than with another temporary patch. Second, we've started seeing a new issue where Excel crashes and loses unsaved work when people open large spreadsheets, which is particularly affecting the Finance team's day-to-day work — this is being investigated now. Separately, we've noticed that some Finance laptops have become noticeably slower to get to a usable desktop after signing in, following a recent security update; we believe the update is the likely cause and are working to confirm and fix it, without needing to remove the security protections it provides.

None of these open items are affecting the majority of staff, but each has a real impact on the people experiencing it, and we're not treating them as minor. We'll provide a further update once the printer replacement, the Excel issue, and the Finance startup slowdown each have a confirmed fix or a clear timeline.
