Date: 2026-08-12
Status: Active — proactive notice

We're aware that a growing number of staff are having trouble signing in this morning, including some being temporarily locked out of their account. This is a known issue and our team is actively working on it.

If you're still locked out after that, please contact the Service Desk with your username and the time the issue started, rather than continuing to retry.

We don't yet have a confirmed fix time, so we won't promise one — but we will send you an update by 12:00 PM today, even if the issue is still being worked on at that point.

Thank you for your patience — your account and files are safe, and there is no action needed beyond the steps above. We'll keep you posted as soon as we know more.
