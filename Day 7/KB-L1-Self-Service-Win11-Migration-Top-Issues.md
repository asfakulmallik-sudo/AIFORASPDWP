# Self-Service Help: Top Win11 Migration Issues

Version: v1.0
Date: 2026-08-12
Status: Draft
Audience: FinBridge staff — Win11 migration

---

## I can't sign in / my account is locked (AVD)

Don't worry, your account and files are safe. Try this first:

1. Stop trying to sign in and wait 15 minutes — trying repeatedly can make the lock last longer.
2. Restart your device once, then try signing in again.

**Contact the Service Desk if:** you're still locked out after waiting 15 minutes and restarting once. Share your username, the exact time it started, how many times you tried to sign in, and whether this has happened before this week.

---

## The floor 3 printer won't work

1. Sign out and sign back in once — this often resets the printer connection automatically.
2. If you need to print urgently, use a printer on another floor as a temporary workaround.

**Contact the Service Desk if:** the printer still doesn't work after signing back in, or you have an urgent print job. Share which printer/floor, any error message shown, and whether colleagues nearby are affected too.

---

## My OneDrive files seem to be missing

Your files are very likely still safe and just haven't shown up yet.

1. Check the OneDrive icon near the clock (bottom right) — click it to see if it says "syncing."
2. Open OneDrive in a web browser (not the desktop folder) and check there — files often appear online before they appear on your device.

**Contact the Service Desk immediately if:** your files aren't visible within 15 minutes, or you need them urgently. Share the file/folder name, when you last saw it, your deadline, and whether OneDrive shows a red or orange warning icon.
